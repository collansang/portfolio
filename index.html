How we'd work together

Four stages, start to delivery.

01 - Tell me what you need

Understand the brief

Share what you're looking for, where the design will be used, your preferred style, deadline, and any references or materials you already have.

02 - Plan the design

Set the direction

We clarify the direction, content, visual style, and requirements before the actual design work begins.

003 - Design & refine

Build the system

I develop the design and make revisions based on your feedback until everything is clear, polished, and ready to use.

04 - Final delivery

Hand it off

You receive the finished files in the formats you need, prepared for print, social media, digital use, or wherever the design will be used.
